# Research positioning

GeoTwin Sentinel is an executable research artifact at the intersection of trustworthy agentic systems, infrastructure digital twins, healthcare resilience, compound climate/cyber disruption, human–AI decision support, counterfactual reasoning, evidence-grounded decisions, and observability as an audit mechanism.

Its contribution is integration and inspectability: a synthetic spatial graph state feeds specialized deterministic agents; their evidence dependencies affect a versioned trust model; interventions are compared against an exact baseline; and the workflow is correlated through OpenTelemetry. This supports controlled experiments without claiming clinical efficacy, causal validity, or operational deployment.

## Research questions

1. How can agentic digital twins represent compound infrastructure disruptions?
2. How should agents expose evidence dependencies and confidence?
3. How can telemetry-integrity degradation affect intervention ranking?
4. Can observability improve auditability of agentic decision-support workflows?
5. How should human-review policies respond to uncertainty and agent failure?
6. How can counterfactual interventions be compared without overstating causal certainty?

The current repository demonstrates functional mechanisms and testable hypotheses. It has not completed controlled experiments with practitioners, calibrated real-world data, comparative model validation, or a peer-reviewed study. The older [`research/RESEARCH_PLAN.md`](../../research/RESEARCH_PLAN.md) is a proposed study plan, not evidence of completed evaluation.
